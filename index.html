Agentic UI experiments

Experiments · Agentic UI & commerce

Interactive demos of agentic UI and agentic commerce: CRM workflows where an agent proposes changes next to the people who approve them, and shopping flows where an agent handles reorder and checkout with you in the loop.

  • Agentic CRM #1 · Review rate The agent reviews daily rates: it trims one that's too high and raises one that's too low, with a reason and your approval.
  • Agentic CRM #2 · Compliance sweep The agent walks the whole list. Six people stay quiet. Two need you: a chase to send, and a call it will not make.
  • Shop · Dog food reorder Connect Shop Pay, set budget and delivery, and let the agent reorder Luna’s food to your parcel locker.
  • InPost Pay · Price check After a Shop order, the agent connects InPost Pay, finds the same food cheaper, and finishes checkout in the familiar flow.
